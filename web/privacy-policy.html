YoMart Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026

This policy describes data handled by the YoMart shopping app and website. The publisher must fill in the legal business identity and support contact below before publishing this page.

Release setup required: replace “YoMart publisher legal name” with the responsible legal business name and add a monitored support email and business address/contact before submitting this policy to Google Play.

Information we handle

  • Account information: username, email address, password hash, and account timestamps.
  • Shopping and customer support information: cart and favorites stored on the device, product reviews, orders, selected payment method, and delivery name/contact/address.
  • Delivery location: approximate coordinates are sent only if you choose a location on the delivery map and confirm it.
  • Images you submit: payment receipts and other images supported by the app. The backend resizes and converts uploaded images before storage.
  • Technical request data: the hosting provider and API may process connection data such as IP address, request time, and device/browser information in their service logs.
  • Push notification identifier and permission state only when the operator configures OneSignal and you grant notification permission.

Why we use information

We use this information to create and secure accounts, display the catalog, process and deliver physical product orders, accept payment proof where applicable, support customers, prevent abuse, and send opted-in order or service notifications.

Service providers and sharing

Data is processed by the YoMart API and database host. Product, category, brand, and payment-proof images may be stored by the image-storage provider configured by the operator (Cloudinary or a configured persistent server volume). The map uses OpenStreetMap map tiles; tile requests disclose ordinary network connection data to that service. OneSignal receives push identifiers only when push is enabled by the operator. The operator must update this policy if providers change.

We do not sell personal information. Information may be disclosed when required by law or to protect the service and users.

Retention and deletion

When you delete your account, sign-in is disabled and direct profile, contact, and delivery details are removed. Reviews are removed. Order and payment evidence may be retained in anonymized form for business records and reconciliation; payment receipts are no longer available to the deleted customer. The owner must confirm the final retention period and applicable legal requirements before publication. You can request deletion in the app under Profile, or use the external account deletion page. The external form verifies account ownership with the account email and password.

Security and children

Passwords are stored as salted password hashes, and protected API requests require an access token. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. YoMart is a general shopping service and is not designed for children under 13.

Your choices

You can choose whether to grant notification permission and whether to select a map location. You can request account deletion using the methods above. Use the support contact shown in the app for questions about your data.
